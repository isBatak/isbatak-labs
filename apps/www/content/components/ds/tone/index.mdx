A tone shows whether a number went up or down. `tone="positive"` colors it `fg.success` with an up caret,
`tone="negative"` colors it `fg.error` with a down caret, and `tone="neutral"` keeps the inherited color and leaves an
empty slot where the caret would be, so neutral figures line up with the others. Digits use tabular numbers.

## Examples

### Profit and loss

`detectTone` turns a number, or a numeric string, into a tone: `positive` above zero, `negative` below and `neutral` for
zero or anything that isn't a number. Show the absolute value; the caret carries the sign.

### Plain

`plain` drops the color from the root, so only what you color yourself, here the indicator, changes.
