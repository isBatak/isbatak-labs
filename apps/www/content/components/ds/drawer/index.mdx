## Examples

### Basic

A bottom drawer with a grabber. Drag it down or press Escape to close it.

<Example id="drawer" />

### Swipe direction

Set `placement` to `start`, `end`, `top` or `bottom` to choose the edge the drawer slides in from. `Drawer.Root` turns
it into Zag's `swipeDirection`, so the drawer swipes back toward the same edge. `start` and `end` follow the text
direction: turn on right-to-left in the preview toolbar and they swap sides.

<Example id="drawer/swipe-direction" />

### Float

Set `float` to float the drawer inside the viewport, with a gap from the edge and rounded corners on every side. It
works with every swipe direction.

<Example id="drawer/float" />

### Snap points

`snapPoints` lists the heights the drawer can rest at, as fractions of the viewport or as lengths like `"20rem"`.
`defaultSnapPoint` picks where it opens. A snap point never exceeds the content size, so give the content a height when
you snap to a fraction. The content gets `data-expanded` at its full height, which this example uses to let the list
scroll only then.

<Example id="drawer/snap-points" />

### Modal

Set `modal`, `trapFocus`, `preventScroll` and `closeOnInteractOutside` to `false` to keep the page usable while the
drawer is open. Leave out `Drawer.Backdrop` so it doesn't cover the page.

<Example id="drawer/non-modal" />

### Controlled

Pass `open` and `onOpenChange` to open and close the drawer from outside and follow its state. Anything that sets the
state opens or closes it, so it needs no `Drawer.Trigger`.

<Example id="drawer/controlled" />

### Scrollable

Long content scrolls inside `Drawer.Body`, so the header and footer stay in view. Swiping only starts once the body is
scrolled to its edge.

<Example id="drawer/scrollable" />

### No drag area

Add `data-no-drag` to an element inside the content to stop drags from starting on it, here a row of swatches that
scrolls sideways.

<Example id="drawer/no-drag-area" />

### Non draggable

Pass `draggable={false}` to `Drawer.Content` so drags on the content don't move the drawer. The grabber stays a drag
handle; leave it out to turn off swipe to dismiss.

<Example id="drawer/non-draggable" />

### Indent background

Wrap the page in `Drawer.Stack` and `Drawer.Indent` to shrink it back behind the drawer, over a
`Drawer.IndentBackground`. The indent follows the swipe, so the page grows again as you swipe the drawer away.

<Example id="drawer/indent-background" />

### Stacked

Open a drawer from inside another and the one below shrinks and peeks out behind it, through the `--nested-drawers`
variable. Closing the top drawer returns to the one behind it. Only the first drawer renders a backdrop.

<Example id="drawer/stacking" />

### Multiple triggers

Give each `Drawer.Trigger` a `value` to share one drawer. `triggerValue` on the drawer API says which one opened it, and
`onTriggerValueChange` reports it.

<Example id="drawer/multiple-triggers" />

### Using the API

Create the drawer with `useDrawer` and pass it to `Drawer.RootProvider` to drive it from anywhere: `setOpen()` and
`setSnapPoint()` change it, and `open`, `snapPoint` and `triggerValue` read it. `useDrawer` takes Zag's `swipeDirection`
directly, and the recipe styles the drawer from it.

<Example id="drawer/root-provider" />
