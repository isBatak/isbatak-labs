A native `button` styled with the `button` recipe. `loading` replaces the label with a spinner and disables the button,
and `loadingText` shows text next to the spinner instead. `ButtonGroup` and `ButtonPropsProvider` share props with every
button inside them. For icon-only buttons, see [Icon button](/ds/components/icon-button) and
[Close button](/ds/components/close-button).

### Accessible when disabled

A natively disabled button can't take focus, so keyboard and screen reader users can miss it and never learn why it's
unavailable. `accessibleWhenDisabled` uses `aria-disabled` instead: the button stays focusable and is announced as
disabled, its styles still follow `_disabled`, and clicks are blocked.
