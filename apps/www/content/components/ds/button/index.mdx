A native `button` styled with the `button` recipe. `loading` replaces the label with a spinner and disables the button,
and `loadingText` shows text next to the spinner instead. The package also exports `IconButton`, `ButtonGroup` and
`CloseButton`, and `ButtonPropsProvider` sets shared props for every button inside it.
