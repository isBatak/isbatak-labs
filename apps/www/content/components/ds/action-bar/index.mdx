Built on Ark UI's Popover, kept open while there's a selection. It doesn't trap focus or close when you click outside
it, so people can keep selecting items. Pass `open` to show it, and `onOpenChange` to hear when it's closed with Escape
or `ActionBar.CloseTrigger`.

## Usage

<Usage />

```tsx
<ActionBar.Root open={selected.length > 0}>
  <Portal>
    <ActionBar.Positioner>
      <ActionBar.Content>
        <ActionBar.SelectionTrigger />
        <ActionBar.Separator />
        <Button />
        <ActionBar.CloseTrigger />
      </ActionBar.Content>
    </ActionBar.Positioner>
  </Portal>
</ActionBar.Root>
```

## Examples
