Built on Ark UI's Rating Group. Set `count` to the number of stars and `defaultValue` or `value` to the rating. Arrow
keys move the rating, and `RatingGroup.HiddenInput` sends it with a form.

## Usage

<Usage />

```tsx
<RatingGroup.Root count={5} defaultValue={3}>
  <RatingGroup.Label />
  <RatingGroup.HiddenInput />
  <RatingGroup.Control>
    <RatingGroup.Items />
  </RatingGroup.Control>
</RatingGroup.Root>
```

`RatingGroup.Items` renders a `RatingGroup.Item` with a `RatingGroup.ItemIndicator` for each star. The indicator draws
the icon twice, a muted one behind and a colored one in front, and clips the front one for a half rating. Use the parts
on their own to render something other than the indicator in an item.

### Custom icon

Pass your own icon to draw instead of the star: the `icon` prop in React, the `icon` slot in Vue, the `icon` snippet in
Svelte and the `icon` function in Solid. Vue, Svelte and Solid hand you a `data-bg` or `data-fg` attribute to spread on
the icon, so the recipe can tell the two copies apart.
