Built on Ark UI's Tour. Steps are defined in `useTour`, and a `tooltip` step points at the element its `target` returns.
`Tour.Actions` passes the current step's `actions` to you, and `Tour.ActionTrigger` turns each one into a button. With
no children, `Tour.CloseTrigger` shows a close button.

## Usage

<Usage />

```tsx
<Tour.Root tour={tour}>
  <Portal>
    <Tour.Backdrop />
    <Tour.Spotlight />
    <Tour.Positioner>
      <Tour.Content>
        <Tour.Arrow>
          <Tour.ArrowTip />
        </Tour.Arrow>
        <Tour.CloseTrigger />
        <Tour.Title />
        <Tour.Description />
        <Tour.Control>
          <Tour.ProgressText />
          <Tour.Actions>{(actions) => <Tour.ActionTrigger />}</Tour.Actions>
        </Tour.Control>
      </Tour.Content>
    </Tour.Positioner>
  </Portal>
</Tour.Root>
```

## Examples

### Step types

Set a step's `type`. A `dialog` step has no target and opens in the middle of the screen. A `tooltip` step points at its
target. A `floating` step sits at its `placement`, such as `bottom-end`, and doesn't block the page.

<Example id="tour/step-types" />

### Wait for a click

Give a step an `effect` to wait for something before moving on. Here `waitForEvent` waits for a click on the target,
then calls `next`. Call `show` to show the step while it waits.
