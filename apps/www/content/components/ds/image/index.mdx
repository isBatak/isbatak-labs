A styled `img`. `fit` and `align` map to `object-fit` and `object-position`; `fit` defaults to `cover`. The `image`
recipe adds a faint inset [image outline](/ds/styles/image-outlines) so pale pictures keep their edge; set
`outlineStyle="none"` to turn it off.
