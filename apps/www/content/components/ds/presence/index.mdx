Ark UI's Presence keeps an element in the page until its exit animation ends, then hides or removes it. It sets
`data-state="open"` or `"closed"`, so `_open` and `_closed` styles can animate it. Overlays use it for their content and
backdrop. It has no styles.

Set `lazyMount` to render nothing until the first time it's present, and `unmountOnExit` to remove it again once it
closes.

## Usage

<Usage />

```tsx
<Presence present={open} lazyMount unmountOnExit className={css({ _open: {}, _closed: {} })}>
  Content
</Presence>
```
