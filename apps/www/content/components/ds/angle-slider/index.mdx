Built on Ark UI's AngleSlider. Drag the thumb around the dial, or focus it and use the arrow keys; Home and End jump to
0 and 360. The value text shows the angle with a degree sign unless you give it children. Markers mark angles on the
dial and get `data-state` `under-value`, `at-value` or `over-value`, so the ones the thumb has passed are tinted.
