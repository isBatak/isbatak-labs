`columns` sets equal columns and `minChildWidth` fits as many as there's room for. Unlike Panda's built-in pattern,
`Grid` adds no gap unless you set one. `GridItem` takes `colSpan`, `rowSpan`, `colStart`, `colEnd`, `rowStart`, `rowEnd`
and `area`.
