Built on Ark UI's Scroll Area. Give `ScrollArea.Root` a height or width, put the content in `ScrollArea.Viewport` and
`ScrollArea.Content`, and add a `ScrollArea.Scrollbar` for each direction that scrolls. With no children,
`ScrollArea.Scrollbar` renders its `ScrollArea.Thumb`.

## Usage

<Usage />

```tsx
<ScrollArea.Root>
  <ScrollArea.Viewport>
    <ScrollArea.Content />
  </ScrollArea.Viewport>
  <ScrollArea.Scrollbar>
    <ScrollArea.Thumb />
  </ScrollArea.Scrollbar>
  <ScrollArea.Corner />
</ScrollArea.Root>
```
