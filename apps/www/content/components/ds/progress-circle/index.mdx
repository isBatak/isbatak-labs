A progress circle shows how much of a task is done in a small, round space. It is the circular form of `Progress`. Leave
the value out, or pass `null`, for an indeterminate spinner.

## Usage

<Usage />

```tsx
<ProgressCircle.Root>
  <ProgressCircle.Label />
  <ProgressCircle.Circle>
    <ProgressCircle.Track />
    <ProgressCircle.Range />
  </ProgressCircle.Circle>
  <ProgressCircle.ValueText />
</ProgressCircle.Root>
```
