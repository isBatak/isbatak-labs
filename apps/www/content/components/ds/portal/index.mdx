Overlays like Dialog, Popover and Menu put their positioner in a `Portal`, so a parent with `overflow: hidden` or its
own stacking context can't clip or cover them. It has no styles.

Each framework uses its own portal:

- **React and Svelte:** Ark UI's Portal. Pass `container` to render somewhere other than the end of the body, or
  `disabled` to render in place.
- **Vue:** a thin wrapper around Vue's `Teleport`. `to` defaults to `body`; `disabled` and `defer` pass through.
- **Solid:** a wrapper around Solid's Portal that only mounts when it has something to render. A portal that's always
  mounted would be hidden from screen readers by nested overlays.

## Usage

<Usage />

```tsx
<Portal>
  <div>Rendered at the end of the body</div>
</Portal>
```
