Built on Ark UI's Steps. Set `count` on the root, give each `Steps.Item` and `Steps.Content` its `index`, and move
between steps with `Steps.PrevTrigger` and `Steps.NextTrigger`. With no children, `Steps.Indicator` shows the step's
number, then a check once the step is complete.

## Usage

<Usage />

```tsx
<Steps.Root count={3}>
  <Steps.List>
    <Steps.Item index={0}>
      <Steps.Indicator />
      <Steps.Title />
      <Steps.Separator />
    </Steps.Item>
  </Steps.List>
  <Steps.Content index={0} />
  <Steps.CompletedContent />
  <Steps.PrevTrigger />
  <Steps.NextTrigger />
</Steps.Root>
```
