Built on Ark UI's HoverCard. It opens after a short delay on hover or focus, and `openDelay` and `closeDelay` change the
timing.

<Usage />

## Examples

### Sizes

Set `size` to `xs`, `sm`, `md` or `lg` to change the padding. The default is `md`.

<Example id="hover-card/sizes" />

### Placement

Pass `positioning={{ placement }}` to choose which side of the trigger the card opens on.

<Example id="hover-card/placement" />

### Delay

Set `openDelay` and `closeDelay` in milliseconds to change how long the card waits.

<Example id="hover-card/delay" />

### Controlled

Pass `open` and `onOpenChange` to follow and change whether the card is open.

<Example id="hover-card/controlled" />
