title: Loader
description: Swap content for a spinner or loading text while work is in progress.
category: Feedback
frameworks: [react, vue, solid, svelte]
links:
  source: packages/ui/react/src/components/loader
---

`Button` uses it for its loading state. With `text`, it shows the spinner next to the text. Without it, the spinner sits
over the children, which stay hidden but keep their size. Set `visible={false}` to show the children.
