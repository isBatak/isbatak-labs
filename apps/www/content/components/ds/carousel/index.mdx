Built on Ark UI's Carousel. Pass the number of slides to `Carousel.Root` as `count` and give each `Carousel.Item` its
`index`. With no children, `Carousel.PrevTrigger` and `Carousel.NextTrigger` render a ghost icon button with a chevron,
and `Carousel.Indicators` renders one indicator per page.

## Usage

<Usage />

```tsx
<Carousel.Root count={3}>
  <Carousel.ItemGroup>
    <Carousel.Item index={0} />
  </Carousel.ItemGroup>
  <Carousel.Control>
    <Carousel.PrevTrigger />
    <Carousel.Indicators />
    <Carousel.NextTrigger />
  </Carousel.Control>
</Carousel.Root>
```

### Autoplay

Pass `autoPlay` as `true` or `{ delay }` in milliseconds. `Carousel.AutoplayTrigger` pauses and resumes it, and
`Carousel.AutoplayIndicator` shows its children while playing and its `fallback` while paused.

<Example id="carousel/with-autoplay" />
