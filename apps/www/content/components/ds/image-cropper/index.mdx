Built on Ark UI's ImageCropper. Drag the selection to move it, drag a handle to resize it, scroll or pinch to zoom, and
drag outside the selection to pan the image. With the selection focused, arrow keys move it, Alt+arrows resize it and
`+`/`-` zoom. Leave `Selection` empty and it renders the eight resize handles and the rule-of-thirds grid for you; pass
children to place your own.

## Usage

<Usage />

```tsx
<ImageCropper.Root>
  <ImageCropper.Viewport>
    <ImageCropper.Image src={src} alt={alt} />
    <ImageCropper.Selection>
      <ImageCropper.Handle placement="nw" />
      <ImageCropper.Handle placement="n" />
      <ImageCropper.Handle placement="ne" />
      <ImageCropper.Handle placement="e" />
      <ImageCropper.Handle placement="se" />
      <ImageCropper.Handle placement="s" />
      <ImageCropper.Handle placement="sw" />
      <ImageCropper.Handle placement="w" />
      <ImageCropper.Grid axis="horizontal" />
      <ImageCropper.Grid axis="vertical" />
    </ImageCropper.Selection>
  </ImageCropper.Viewport>
</ImageCropper.Root>
```
