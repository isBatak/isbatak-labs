Built on Ark UI's Collapsible. `Collapsible.Trigger` opens and closes `Collapsible.Content`, which animates its height.
With no children, `Collapsible.Indicator` shows a chevron that turns when the content opens. For several sections that
open and close together, use Accordion.

## Usage

<Usage />

```tsx
<Collapsible.Root>
  <Collapsible.Trigger />
  <Collapsible.Content />
</Collapsible.Root>
```
