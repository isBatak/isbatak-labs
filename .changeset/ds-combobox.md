The `combobox` recipe is restyled to match Select: the list, items, groups and empty state share its look, the trigger
chevron turns while the list is open, and the input takes the `input` recipe's variants. It gains `itemContent` and
`itemDescription` slots for two-line items, loses its duplicate `empty` slot, and now shows the invalid border in every
variant.
