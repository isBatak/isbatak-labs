Add the grain preset: SVG `feTurbulence` film grain as an `assets.grain` token with a light and a dark tile, a
`sizes.grain` token and a `grain` layer style. Size, frequency and speck strength per mode are options. The analog theme
now takes its grain from it instead of inlined PNGs, and no longer exports `grainDark` and `grainLight`.
