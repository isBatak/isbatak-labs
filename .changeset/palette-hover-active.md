Every color palette has `hover` and `active` tokens: translucent fills (`500/12` and `500/20` light, `400/16` and
`400/10` dark) that tint any background they sit on. Ghost and outline buttons, line tabs and folder tabs use them for
hover and press, and folder tabs gain a hover and press fill on unselected tabs.
