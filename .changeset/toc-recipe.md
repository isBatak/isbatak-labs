Add a `toc` slot recipe for Ark UI's table of contents. The `line` variant puts a bar on a border at the start of the
list, and the `track` variant styles a `track` slot, an SVG line that steps in for nested items, with a dot at the end
of the active stretch. `size` takes `sm` or `md`, and `--toc-indent` sets how far each level is indented.
