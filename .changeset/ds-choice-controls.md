Choice controls in every framework: Checkmark, Radiomark, Checkbox (with `Group`), CheckboxCard and RadioGroup are new,
and Switch comes to Vue, Svelte and Solid with `Indicator`, `ThumbIndicator` and `RootProvider`. Segment Group gets a
`tinted` variant, which colours the selection with `colorPalette`, and an `icon` variant for icon-only segments.

Segment Group drops its `variant` prop: `enclosed` is now its only look.
