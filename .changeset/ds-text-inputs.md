Text inputs in every framework: Input, Textarea, Field, Fieldset (with `Content`) and InputGroup with `InputElement` and
`InputAddon`. Field gets an `OptionalText` part that shows "(optional)" when the field isn't required.

Input, Textarea, NumberInput, PinInput, Combobox, DatePicker, DateInput and InputAddon get a `plain` variant without a
border or background. The input recipe now pads itself for InputGroup start and end elements.
