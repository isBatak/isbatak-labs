Add a video player machine and its Panda slot recipe. It drives the native `<video>` element: play, seek, volume, mute,
loop, picture in picture and fullscreen, with seek and volume sliders built from track, range, thumb and input parts
(pointer dragging and keyboard steps), a seek preview with a thumbnail, keyboard shortcuts, and `variant` and `size`
recipe variants.
